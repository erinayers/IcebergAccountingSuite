Your books, on your computer.

Iceberg is a free, open-source accounting suite for individuals and small businesses: double-entry books, invoicing, financial reports, and bank reconciliation - all in one encrypted file that never leaves your machine.

What Iceberg does

Ledger

Record every transaction against a chart of accounts with debit and credit sides, receipts attached, search as you type, and a running register per account.

Invoices

Build invoices from your services, track Due, Overdue, Paid, Refunded and Canceled with a full history, and print them to PDF.

Reports

Income statement, income statement by month, balance sheet, and cash-flow statement for any period, exportable to CSV for your accountant.

Reconciliation

Tick off transactions against your bank or card statement and finish only when the difference is zero.

Safe saves

Every save is atomic and verified, and a timestamped backup is kept automatically. Restore any of the last twenty from inside the app.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Your database is encrypted with a key file only you hold.

Who it is for

Sole traders, freelancers, landlords, and small service businesses who want proper double-entry books without subscription software, and who are comfortable with a desktop application and a simple chart of accounts. If you send invoices for services, pay expenses from one or two bank accounts, and need year-end reports for your accountant, Iceberg fits.

What it is not (yet)

  • Single user, single machine. One person works on a database at a time; there is no sync, multi-user access, or mobile app.
  • No payroll, inventory valuation, or multi-currency. Product inventory accounts exist, but stock levels are not tracked.
  • No bank feeds. Transactions are entered by hand or imported from CSV, then reconciled against statements.
  • Sales tax is a single rate per database, applied to taxable services.
  • Windows 10/11 and Linux. macOS builds are possible from source but are not published yet.
